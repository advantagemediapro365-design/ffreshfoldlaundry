Hi \[Developer Name\],

I want to give you a complete overview of the Fresh Fold Pickup &
Delivery project before we discuss the business terms separately.

For this first phase, you will be working directly with me, Tony, and my
assistant. I will be providing direction, approvals, business decisions,
pricing rules, service logic, and final review. My assistant will help
with content support, SEO support, data entry, checking links, reviewing
pages, testing forms, and organizing anything needed to help you move
faster.

The main thing I need you focused on is the technical Wix build, the
website structure, the customer booking system, the delivery workflow,
the driver-side workflow, and the backend/admin setup.

This project is not just a simple Wix website. It needs to function as a
working beta operations platform for a laundry pickup and delivery
business.

Fresh Fold Pickup & Delivery is both:

1.  A laundry service company

2.  A delivery/logistics company

The website needs to reflect both sides of the business.

Customers must be able to book laundry pickup, choose laundry
preferences, see pricing, understand delivery fees, add a driver tip,
subscribe to laundry plans, receive order updates, and rate the service
after delivery.

Drivers must be able to receive assigned orders, follow pickup and
delivery procedures, upload proof photos, leave internal notes, and rate
or comment on customer/order issues.

Admin must be able to view orders, assign drivers, update statuses,
review notes, manage customer preferences, track tips, and operate the
workflow from inside Wix as much as possible.

This first version does not need to be perfect or fully automated like a
custom app, but it does need to be functional, organized, and built
correctly so it can be improved later.

# Delivery Pricing System 

The delivery system must be built into the booking flow as an estimated
logistics fee.

Customer Types

One-time customer:

-   base fee: \$3.80

-   mileage: \$1.75 per round-trip mile Weekly route customer:

-   base fee: \$2.50

-   mileage: \$1.50 per round-trip mile Subscriber:

-   base fee: \$0

-   mileage: \$1.25 per round-trip mile Commercial account:

-   custom

Delivery Formula

Laundry Processing Fee:

Estimated pounds x selected laundry rate Round Trip Miles: One-way miles
x 2 Mileage Fee:

Round-trip miles x customer mileage rate Delivery Fee:

Base fee + mileage fee + condition adjustments Customer Total:

Laundry fee + delivery fee + add-ons + specialty items + tip - discounts

Condition Adjustments

Normal:

0%

Heavy rain:

+10%

Snow advisory:

+15%

Winter storm:

+25%

Emergency route:

+35% to +50% High demand: +10% to +25% After-hours:

+15% to +25%

Weekend / holiday:

+10% to +30%

Off-peak / flexible pickup:

-5% to -15%

Customer-Facing Delivery Message

Use this concept:

Fresh Fold separates laundry service from delivery logistics.
Non-subscriber orders include a base dispatch fee plus mileage.
Subscribers receive member delivery pricing with no base dispatch fee
and a fixed mileage rate. Customers may leave an optional driver tip and
rate their pickup and delivery experience after the order is completed.

No Automatic Free Delivery

There should be no automatic free delivery.

Free or discounted delivery should only apply through:

-   coupon

-   promotion

-   subscription benefit

-   approved commercial agreement

-   manual admin approval

Driver Pickup Workflow

Driver must:

1.  Arrive at pickup location.

2.  Confirm customer name.

3.  Confirm pickup address.

4.  Locate dirty laundry.

5.  Take a photo of where laundry was left.

6.  Count bags.

7.  Attach customer/order label.

8.  Scan or enter order/QR code if available.

9.  Mark order as picked up.

10. Leave internal note if needed.

11. Trigger customer pickup notification.

Driver Drop-Off Workflow

Driver must:

1.  Confirm order ID.

2.  Confirm customer name.

3.  Confirm delivery address.

4.  Confirm bag count.

5.  Take photo before leaving order.

6.  Place clean laundry in instructed location.

7.  Mark order as delivered.

8.  Leave internal delivery note if needed.

9.  Trigger customer delivery notification.

10. Trigger customer review request.
